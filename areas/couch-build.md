# Couch build (personal project, futon-mattress DIY couch)

Brian's own build, not a customer job. Drawings are in the CAD app's Projects list
(*Couch — side view ...* and *Couch — plan (overhead)*, see `areas/cad-app.md`).
Plans and cut lists were written in claude.ai and live with Brian, not in this repo.

## Decided

- Built around a full futon mattress (54 x 75 in, folds to 27 in seat + 27 in back).
- Frame 75 x 27 in of 2x4, legs 8 in front / 6 in rear, so the seat tilts 4 degrees.
  Seat height 17 in to the top of the compressed mattress, arms 25 in.
- **Back hinges** on a 3/8 in bolt through each upright and rear arm post. Uprights sit
  outside the arm posts so they can swing past. Back is 28 in long.
- **Settings:** 105 / 120 / 135 / 150 degrees on a prop, flat (180) with the back lying
  on tail rails.
- **Prop (final, replaces earlier versions):** two 2x4 x 18-1/2 in props hinge on two
  20 in tail rails bolted to the back face of the rear rail (top edge 9-5/8 in above the
  floor). Hinge bolt 11-1/4 in behind the rear rail and 7-7/8 in above the floor. The top
  end of each prop hooks a 3/8 in bolt pin through the back upright at 13.9 / 18.0 / 22.3 /
  26.4 in from the pivot. The couch needs about 20 in of clear space behind it.
- **Laptop arm:** an articulating arm Brian already owns mounts to the **seat frame, not
  the back**, so it reaches him at every recline. Receiver pocket (sized to the arm's leg)
  on a 1x6 sled riding a drawer glide on a 1x8 side panel, one on each side.

## Superseded (do not use)

- Strut and notched rack beside the seat: a hand-pinch hazard.
- Prop hooking a crossbar behind the rear rail: at 135 degrees and below the prop tip goes
  through the floor, and no length fixes it.

## Open

- Back width as built is unknown. The drawing assumes 78 in between uprights (85 in overall).
- Arm leg size (thickness, width, insertion length) is needed to size the receiver pocket.
- Pin 4 sits 1-1/2 in from the top of the upright; check the wood there.
- Every setting must be load-tested before use.
