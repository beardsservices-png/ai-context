# Couch build (personal project, futon-mattress DIY couch)

Brian's own build, not a customer job. Drawings are in the CAD app's Projects list
(*Couch side view (bed-style, head to toe)* and *Couch plan view (bed-style, head to toe)*,
see `areas/cad-app.md`). Plans and cut lists were written in claude.ai and live with Brian
and in Drive (*Couch build* folder), not in this repo.

## ORIENTATION (corrected 14 Oct, read first)

**Brian's build is bed-style: the 75 in length runs head to toe and the 27 in is the width.**
The back/head end is the narrow end. It is a chaise/bed, not a couch you sit sideways on.
Earlier drafts treated the 75 in as the couch front, with the back along it. All of that was
wrong. Dimensions themselves are correct.

## Decided

- Thin futon-style mattress. Frame 75 x 27 in of 2x4, 75 in head to toe, 27 in wide.
- Legs 6 in at the head, 7 in middle, 8 in at the foot, so the seat slopes toward the foot
  (rail top = 9.5 in + 2 in over the 75 in). No arms drawn.
- **Back hinges at the head end**: pivot at slat-top level, 1.75 in from the head end, on a
  3/8 in bolt through each upright. The uprights sit **outside the long rails** so they swing
  past. Back is 28 in long, 27 in between uprights, about 34 in overall (4 slats 1x6 x 34).
- **Settings** are the angle between the back and the seat measured at the head end.
- **Settings:** 105 / 120 / 135 / 150 degrees on a prop, flat (180) with the back lying
  on tail rails.
- **Prop (final, same geometry, now behind the head end):** two 2x4 x 18-1/2 in props hinge
  on two 20 in tail rails that run straight out behind the head end (x -20 to 0, top edge
  9.5 to 6 in above the floor, bolted to the head end rail, 6 in tail legs). Hinge bolt
  13 in behind the pivot and 2.5 in below it. The top end of each prop hooks a 3/8 in bolt pin
  through the back upright at 13.9 / 18.0 / 22.3 / 26.4 in from the pivot. Flat (180) the
  back lies on the tail rails. **About 20 in of clear space is needed behind the head end.**
- **Laptop arm:** an articulating arm Brian already owns mounts to the **seat frame, not
  the back**, so it reaches him at every recline. Receiver pocket (sized to the arm's leg)
  on a 1x6 sled riding a drawer glide on a 1x8 side panel, one on each long side.

## Superseded (do not use)

- Strut and notched rack beside the seat: a hand-pinch hazard.
- Prop hooking a crossbar behind the rear rail: at 135 degrees and below the prop tip goes
  through the floor, and no length fixes it.

## Open

- Back width as built is unknown. The drawing assumes 27 in between uprights (34 in overall).
- The tail rails bolt only to a 1.5 in head end rail and carry the prop reaction: bolt
  through, add a diagonal brace or a cleat to the stringer.
- Legs 6/7/8 in must slope along the length. If Brian's 8 in legs all sit on one long rail
  the surface tilts sideways; swap them.
- Arm leg size (thickness, width, insertion length) is needed to size the receiver pocket.
- Pin 4 sits 1-1/2 in from the top of the upright; check the wood there.
- Every setting must be load-tested before use.
